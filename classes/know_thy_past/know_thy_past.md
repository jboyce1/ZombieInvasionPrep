**Focus**: Using patterns and evidence to understand outbreaks and guide emergency response

**Activities**: Plague Tag, Common Source Challenge, Plagues of Yore, and the Triangulation Challenge

## Essential Question

**How can evidence from past outbreaks help people understand and respond to future emergencies?**

## Objectives

* Explain how contact and disease timelines affect the spread of an outbreak.
* Distinguish between patterns caused by communicable disease and a common source.
* Use evidence to investigate an outbreak without assuming the answer in advance.
* Research a historical disease and communicate useful findings to a team.
* Apply leadership, teamwork, and After Action Review practices during timed challenges.
* Connect rescue, casualty-care, radio, and mapping skills to emergency response decisions.

## Skills

* Tie and test a Swiss seat.
* Check a casualty for shock.
* Transport a casualty.
* Define a search area using radio-wave propagation.
* Create and apply a sling.
* Create and apply a splint.
