**Focus**: Using mathematical models to test decisions during a fictional zombie emergency

**Activities**: Mathematics in Zombieland project, Zombielympics preparation, presentations, and daily After Action Reviews

## Essential Question

**How can a mathematical model help a team make and defend decisions when the situation is changing and the stakes are high?**

## Objectives

* Translate a fictional outbreak into defined populations, rates, and state variables.
* Explain what a model shows and where its assumptions limit the conclusion.
* Test policy choices by changing justified rates and comparing results.
* Develop a 240-minute response plan supported by mathematical evidence.
* Produce authentic primary-source documents that communicate the scenario and response.
* Organize independent practice for the Zombielympics by sharing expertise across teams.
* Use daily After Action Reviews to improve plans, roles, products, and team conduct.

## Culminating Expectations

This module is the transition from instructor-led practice to team independence. Teams are responsible for assigning work, finding previously taught resources, teaching one another, asking precise questions, managing disagreements, and producing evidence of their decisions.

Respect for people is part of performance. High stress does not excuse contempt, humiliation, exclusion, or abandoning a teammate.
