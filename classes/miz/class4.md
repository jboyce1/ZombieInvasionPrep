# M4 Class 4 — Interpret, Complete, and Rehearse

**Module**: Mathematics in Zombieland

**Focus**: completing the evidence chain, rehearsing the presentation, and closing skill gaps

---

## Essential Question

**Can the team defend every important claim with evidence from its scenario and model?**

## Agenda

### 0945–1120

* **0945–0950** — Launch from the Class 3 AAR
* **0950–1100** — Final production, rehearsal, and Zombielympics preparation
* **1100–1110** — Internal team AAR
* **1110–1120** — Facilitated cross-team AAR led by team leaders

---

# Work Block

### `0950–1100`

Complete the connections among the primary source, rates, graphs, policy, and conclusion. A polished display cannot replace a missing explanation.

Use the rubric to check that the team has:

* interpreted the uncontrolled graph and identified meaningful points
* documented and compared policy alternatives
* issued an authentic official document or order enacting the selected response
* interpreted the changed graph and connected changes to particular policies
* explained the most important action supported by the model
* identified preparations and first steps humanity could take
* written a conclusion explaining what the team learned
* rehearsed so every member can explain the work and answer questions
* identified remaining Zombielympics weaknesses and practiced them deliberately

Before declaring the project finished, have someone who did not create a section check whether its claim, evidence, and explanation agree.
