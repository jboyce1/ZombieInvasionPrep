# M4 Class 2 — Define and Build the Model

**Module**: Mathematics in Zombieland

**Focus**: defining the scenario, rates, state variables, and uncontrolled outbreak

---

## Essential Question

**Do the numbers in the model describe the same outbreak as the team's story?**

## Agenda

### 0945–1120

* **0945–0950** — Team launch and role confirmation
* **0950–1100** — Independent MIZ work and Zombielympics preparation
* **1100–1110** — Internal team AAR
* **1110–1120** — Facilitated cross-team AAR led by team leaders

---

# Work Block

### `0950–1100`

By the end of this class, the team should be able to show:

* a primary-source scenario defining the population, initial zombie count, timeline, conditions, and zombie attributes
* state variables explained in sentence form
* rates justified by the scenario rather than selected only to produce a preferred graph
* an initial model showing what happens without policy changes
* an interpretation identifying important points on the graph
* assigned Zombielympics roles and evidence that each role practiced

Project roles and Zombielympics specialties do not have to match. Coordinate beyond your Z-Team: mapping specialists should work with other mapping specialists, knot specialists with knot specialists, medical specialists with medical specialists, and radio specialists with radio specialists. If a student in the class can teach the skill, use that expertise before requesting instructor help.
