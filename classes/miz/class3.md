# M4 Class 3 — Test Policy Decisions

**Module**: Mathematics in Zombieland

**Focus**: testing feasible interventions and organizing independent skill practice

---

## Essential Question

**Which response changes the outcome, and what does that response cost?**

## Agenda

* **0950–1100** — Independent MIZ work and Zombielympics preparation
* **1100–1110** — Internal team AAR

# Work Block

### `0950–1100`

The team should use the uncontrolled model as a baseline, brainstorm several plausible policies, and document each policy's likely effect on the model's rates and real-world conditions. Consider time, resources, manpower, opportunity costs, existing infrastructure, and the order in which actions could actually occur.

By the end of class, aim to have:

* several documented policy options
* costs, benefits, and feasibility compared
* more than one order of implementation considered
* the most promising policies tested in the spreadsheet
* a defensible policy direction selected from evidence
* continued Zombielympics practice, including cross-team peer teaching

Changing a number is not yet a policy. Explain what people would do, when they would do it, and why that action would change that rate.
