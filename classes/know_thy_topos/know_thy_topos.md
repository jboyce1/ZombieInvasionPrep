# M3 — Know Thy Topos

# Classes

* [Know Thy Topos Class 2 — Securing Supplies and Tracing the Story]({{ '/classes/know_thy_topos/class2' | relative_url }})

**Focus**: Reading the shape of the land and communicating a route clearly enough for someone else to follow

**Activities**: Topographic-map review, Topo Narrative warm-up, Topo Narrative assignment, and peer route tracing

## Essential Question

**How can the landscape become both evidence and language in a story of survival?**

## Objectives

* Recognize major terrain features from contour patterns on a topographic map.
* Use direction, distance, elevation, and terrain sequence to describe movement across land.
* Write a survival narrative whose route can be reconstructed from the evidence in the story.
* Trace and evaluate a route described by another group.
* Assess a possible head injury and identify when emergency help is needed.
* Secure supplies using an appropriate tie-down system.

## Skills

* Assess a head injury.
* Identify terrain features on a topographic map.
* Interpret contour spacing, elevation, direction, and distance.
* Communicate a route through a topo narrative.
* Tie down supplies.
