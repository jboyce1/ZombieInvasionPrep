# M5 Class III — Negotiate and Draft

## Available time: 60–75 minutes

### Independent fieldwork — approximately 15–20 minutes

Choose practice, exploration, or a current-event connection that matters to your development. Work safely and within real credential limits. Add evidence to your field log.

### Convention work — remainder of class

Move from ideas to language the recovering society could actually use. Expect disagreement. The convention must decide when to investigate, debate, amend, compromise, vote, record dissent, or return an issue for more work.

Examine whether separate parts of the emerging agreement contradict one another. Consider whether stated values can be enforced, whether authority has meaningful limits, and whether the response to past abuses creates conditions for future legitimacy.

The teacher will not determine which position is correct or rescue the convention from manageable procedural failure.

### Close — final 5 minutes

Secure the current version of every product and identify what remains possible within one final session. Each person responds:

> How did I behave when another person challenged, changed, delayed, or rejected an idea?
