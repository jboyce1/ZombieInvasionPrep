# M5 Class IV — Decide, Ratify, and Reflect

## Available time: 60–75 minutes

### Final independent fieldwork — approximately 10–15 minutes

Complete one purposeful act of individual practice, exploration, or current-event analysis. Review your four field-log entries and identify how your interests, confidence, or judgment changed.

### Final convention — approximately 35–45 minutes

Complete and present the public account, historical recovery record, reconciliation plan, and governing agreement. Decide how the convention will reach and record its final judgment.

Ratification is not guaranteed. If the agreement is not ratified, preserve the final draft, vote, unresolved objections, and an accurate explanation of why consent was not reached.

### Big Five leadership check-in — approximately 15 minutes

Respond individually with specific evidence from the convention. Traits are descriptive, not good or bad.

1. **Openness:** How did you respond to ambiguity, unfamiliar proposals, and changing plans?
2. **Conscientiousness:** What did other people rely on you to prepare, complete, remember, or document?
3. **Extraversion:** How did you use your voice, and how did you leave room for other voices?
4. **Agreeableness:** How did you cooperate without avoiding necessary disagreement?
5. **Emotional stability:** What did you do when you felt uncertain, frustrated, criticized, ignored, or outvoted?

Then answer:

* What role did you naturally occupy?
* What role did the group actually need from you?
* When did your personality help the convention? When did it make collaboration harder?
* What did you do when instructions were incomplete or the group’s process failed?
* Whose contribution did you initially underestimate?
* Would the recovering society trust you with authority? What evidence supports your answer?
