# M5 Class II — Research and Build

## Available time: 60–75 minutes

### Independent fieldwork — approximately 15–20 minutes

Continue purposeful practice in your own track, explore another course skill, or examine a credible report from the previous seven days. If using news, identify the source, distinguish verified facts from claims, and explain how a specific course skill affected—or could have affected—the outcome.

Update your field log with evidence and a next step.

### Convention work — remainder of class

Continue the constitutional mission using the organization and procedures the class has established. Historical research should move beyond description: determine what a society attempted, who controlled the process, whom it served, what evidence suggests success or failure, and what warning or possibility it offers your new society.

The convention is responsible for noticing missing work, conflicting proposals, unequal participation, weak evidence, and lost time. It may revise its own structure and procedures.

### Close — final 5 minutes

Preserve sources, decisions, drafts, unresolved disagreements, and responsibility for unfinished work. Each person responds in their field log:

> Where did I create clarity, and where did I wait for someone else to create it?
