# M5 Class I — Establish the Convention

## Available time: 60–75 minutes

### Independent fieldwork — approximately 15–20 minutes

Practice within your individual track, explore a skill from another track, or find a credible news report from the previous seven days showing a course skill in use. Record evidence in your field log.

Possible directions include medical readiness, mapping and search planning, emergency communications, public-information verification, weather observation, resource management, food safety, hazard recognition, incident command, or another previously studied skill.

### Convention work — remainder of class

Read [The Long Silence situation report]({{ '/classes/prepare_your_society/prepare_your_society' | relative_url }}#situation-report-the-long-silence) and begin the mission.

The convention must decide what it needs to do, how it will work, how decisions will be preserved, and how it will use the four available sessions. No officers, roles, committees, norms, voting rules, or work plans have been assigned.

The teacher will answer questions about established facts in the scenario. The teacher will not decide how the convention should organize itself.

### Close — final 5 minutes

Preserve the group’s work and decisions in a form that can be recovered next class. Each person adds one observation to their field log:

> What did the group need today, and what did I do when I noticed that need?
