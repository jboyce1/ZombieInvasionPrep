# Should We Call a Zombie a Zombie?

**Focus**: naming, origins, folklore, cultural meaning, and the living dead
**Use for**: Z-Team Assignment 1
**Rubric Categories**: Should We Call a Zombie a Zombie? / Historical Roots of Zombies

This page contains pre-made searches to help you find sources related to the word “zombie,” the historical roots of zombies, and living dead traditions from multiple cultures.

The goal is not to prove that every culture has the same idea of a zombie.

The goal is to investigate how people use names, categories, myths, folklore, disease, fear, and cultural memory to explain the living dead.

# Search Advice

Wikipedia is allowed here as a starting point.

Use it to find names, related terms, categories, and references. Then look for stronger sources from universities, museums, journals, folklore organizations, cultural history organizations, public agencies, or other credible sources.

Do not assume every living dead figure is a zombie.

Compare the similarities and differences. Decide whether the word “zombie” is useful, misleading, too narrow, too broad, or historically loaded.
