# Search Advice

For this section, focus on psychology and behavior.

Useful sources may discuss individuals, groups, children, teenagers, adults, soldiers, disaster survivors, or communities.

Strong sources may help explain:

* what people do immediately when afraid
* what people do when fear lasts a long time
* how anxiety changes behavior
* how trauma changes development
* how fear affects memory and decisions
* how emotions are shaped by context
* how groups react to danger
* how hatred and dehumanization develop
* how age changes the effects of fear and trauma

The best research will help you explain both short-term and long-term effects of a zombie invasion.
