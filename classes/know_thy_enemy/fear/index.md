# Fear Research Library

**Focus**: Fear, anxiety, emotion, threat, and survival
**Use for**: Z-Team Assignment 1
**Rubric Category**: Fear, Anxiety and Survival

This library contains research connected to fear, anxiety, survival, threat response, and human behavior under stress.

Use these sources to help develop your group’s argument about how people might respond to a zombie invasion.

# Research Use

These sources may help with questions about:

* fear
* anxiety
* survival
* danger
* uncertainty
* group behavior
* emotional response
* decision-making
* short-term effects
* long-term effects

Use the research to support your own interpretation. The goal is not to summarize every article. The goal is to find useful ideas, apply them to the zombie scenario, and explain why they matter.
