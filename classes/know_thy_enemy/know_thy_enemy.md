# Skill: Evaluate a Casualty

You encounter a casualty during a zombie invasion.

Your mission is to evaluate the casualty step by step to determine their condition. Choose wisely, or the casualty may not survive.

## Complete Skill Activity

[Open Evaluate a Casualty]({{ '/skills/medical/evaluate_a_casualty/' | relative_url }})

## Assess a Casualty

### 1. Responsiveness

Check whether the casualty responds to:

* Verbal commands
* Physical contact
* Pain

### 2. Breathing

Place your ear near the casualty's mouth while watching their chest.

* Listen for breathing.
* Feel for air leaving the mouth.
* Watch for the chest to rise and fall.

### 3. Pulse

Check for a radial pulse at the wrist.

### 4. Bleeding

Use your hands to carefully sweep the casualty for blood or injuries that may not be immediately visible.

### 5. Shock

Shock occurs when the body's organs and tissues do not receive enough oxygen-rich blood.

A casualty experiencing shock may appear as though they are drowning because their body is not receiving enough oxygen.

### 6. Recovery Position

Before rolling the casualty into the recovery position:

* Feel the back of the neck for signs of injury.
* Watch for possible spinal injuries.
* Attempt to keep the casualty's head and neck aligned while rolling them.
