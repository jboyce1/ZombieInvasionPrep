# Teacher note — Know When to Leave

Mission 2 is intentionally not “Mission 1 in a backpack.” Its central assessment is the changing risk relationship between sheltering and movement during a mass evacuation.

The Word workbook is the detailed teacher-facing record. The PowerPoint is a selective briefing. Students must create two routes that diverge because of two different hazard geographies, research two navigation checkpoints plus a decision checkpoint on each route, and retain an offline map or directions.

Do not reward students for claiming extreme walking, cycling, vehicle, fuel, or carrying performance. Reward transparent assumptions, whole-household accessibility, route-failure thinking, and willingness to revise. A strong plan may conclude that an official shelter-in-place direction makes movement unsafe for one hazard.

Students may not conduct route reconnaissance, contact emergency services or shelters, or disclose home/destination addresses. Grade redacted geographic evidence. Exact household plans remain private.

Suggested pacing: 30 minutes hazards and departure ladder; 45 minutes destinations and routing; 30 minutes transportation math; 30 minutes load/home/communication planning; 30–45 minutes presentation; 10 minutes review.
