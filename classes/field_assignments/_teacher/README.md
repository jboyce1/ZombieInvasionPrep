# Teacher note — Lockdown at Home

This asynchronous mission reframes the older “10 days without power” checklist as a household continuity analysis. It deliberately assesses a bottleneck calculation, precommitment under uncertainty, civic trust, mutual aid, resource allocation, and evidence use.

The public student template separates the evidence record (Slides 2–11) from the family-facing pitch (Slides 12–14). “Teacher-facing” means that students submit the detailed inventory analysis to the teacher but show only the pitch section to family members. Students are repeatedly told not to disclose names, addresses, diagnoses, medications, valuables, weapons, or specific security features.

Suggested pacing: 20 minutes scenario and household profile; 45–60 minutes inventory and calculations; 30 minutes sanitation and decision rules; 20 minutes family briefing; 10 minutes review. Household participation is invited, never required.

Do not grade the wealth or readiness of the household. Grade honest analysis, math, source use, tradeoffs, and the student’s ability to identify gaps. “Not inventoried—adult decision” is valid evidence.

The sanitation language intentionally directs students to official guidance and prohibits practical waste experiments, chemical mixing, burning, burying, or dumping. The security prompt is limited to lawful, passive, nonviolent measures.
