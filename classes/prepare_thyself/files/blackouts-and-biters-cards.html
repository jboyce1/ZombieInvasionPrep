BLACKOUTS & BITERS • RESPONDER CAPABILITIES • CUT ON CARD BORDERS

M1 • Scene Safety & PPE

OWNED BY: MEDICAL / SAR

Check danger, use barriers/PPE, and stop unsafe contact.

Reveals: Can reveal medical exposure, contamination, and unsafe scenes.

Limit: Training never requires entering an unsafe scene.

M2 • Patient Assessment & First Aid

OWNED BY: MEDICAL / SAR

Check responsiveness, breathing, bleeding, heat illness, and basic first-aid needs.

Reveals: Can reveal what is happening to a patient.

Limit: No diagnosis or treatment beyond training.

M3 • CPR & AED

OWNED BY: MEDICAL / SAR

Recognize cardiac arrest, begin CPR, use an AED, and direct someone to call EMS.

Reveals: Can reveal a cardiac emergency.

Limit: Requires a current hands-on course card in real life.

M4 • Triage & EMS Request

OWNED BY: MEDICAL / SAR

Prioritize patients, count needs, request qualified help, and track movement.

Reveals: Can reveal scale and urgency.

Limit: Students do not independently clear or transport patients.

BLACKOUTS & BITERS • RESPONDER CAPABILITIES • CUT ON CARD BORDERS

S1 • CalTopo Common Map

OWNED BY: SAR OPERATIONS

Mark verified locations, hazards, clues, teams, and assignments with times.

Reveals: Can reveal location patterns and missing information.

Limit: Map training does not authorize field deployment.

S2 • Search-Base Documentation

OWNED BY: SAR OPERATIONS

Track team, assignment, equipment, departure, clues, and return time.

Reveals: Can reveal lost accountability and clue connections.

Limit: A team without supervision and communications does not deploy.

S3 • Supervised Drone Recon

OWNED BY: SAR OPERATIONS

Plan objective, airspace, weather, crew, search grid, abort rules, and image log.

Reveals: Can reveal remote visual information.

Limit: The certificated adult PIC approves and controls every real flight.

S4 • Search Planning

OWNED BY: SAR OPERATIONS

Define the search question, safe method, team, communications, and withdrawal trigger.

Reveals: Can reveal where and how to look.

Limit: Use remote or adult-agency options when risk is too high.

BLACKOUTS & BITERS • RESPONDER CAPABILITIES • CUT ON CARD BORDERS

C1 • Amateur Radio Net

OWNED BY: EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS

Establish a directed net, prioritize traffic, relay exactly, and confirm receipt.

Reveals: Can reveal lost, conflicting, or interrupted messages.

Limit: Transmit only within license and assigned-channel rules.

C2 • SKYWARN Observation

OWNED BY: EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS

Report observed weather with time, location, magnitude, and source.

Reveals: Can reveal weather and flood danger.

Limit: Never leave shelter or chase weather for a report.

C3 • Message Log

OWNED BY: EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS

Write source, destination, time, priority, exact message, action, and closure.

Reveals: Can reveal missing or changed information.

Limit: Do not improve, guess, or spread the message.

C4 • Rumor & Public-Info Check

OWNED BY: EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS

Separate verified fact from report, protect privacy, and issue a clear correction.

Reveals: Can reveal deception and misinformation.

Limit: Do not publish victim names or tactical locations.

BLACKOUTS & BITERS • RESPONDER CAPABILITIES • CUT ON CARD BORDERS

L1 • Resource Inventory

OWNED BY: LOGISTICS & RECOVERY

Count, inspect, label, store, issue, recover, and reorder supplies.

Reveals: Can reveal shortages, damage, and misplaced resources.

Limit: Unsafe donations stay quarantined.

L2 • ServSafe Feeding

OWNED BY: LOGISTICS & RECOVERY

Control hygiene, allergens, cross-contamination, temperature, cleaning, and waste.

Reveals: Can reveal food and sanitation threats.

Limit: Food Handler is not medical or manager authority.

L3 • OSHA Hazard Check

OWNED BY: LOGISTICS & RECOVERY

Find hazards and apply elimination, engineering, rules, then PPE.

Reveals: Can reveal unsafe work and contaminated materials.

Limit: OSHA 10 is awareness—not a license for hazardous work.

L4 • Sandbag & Flood Control

OWNED BY: LOGISTICS & RECOVERY

Organize safe filling, carrying, placement, drainage, rotation, and accountability.

Reveals: Can reveal flood-control failures.

Limit: No moving floodwater, electrical hazards, or unsafe lifting.
