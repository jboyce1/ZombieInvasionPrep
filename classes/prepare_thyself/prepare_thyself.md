# Prepare Thyself

**Mission:** build a common emergency-management foundation, select a useful specialty, and begin earning a credential stack that can serve the community.

This module begins immediately after **Know Thy Enemy**. Everyone completes the same common foundation first. After Class 3, each student follows an individual specialization plan through short weekly check-ins while the rest of Zombie Invasion Prep continues.

# Credential timeline

| Milestone | Expected evidence |
|---|---|
| End of Class 1 | FEMA SID/account setup completed according to school and family policy; IS-100 started |
| End of Class 2 | IS-100 certificate or a documented retake plan; IS-700 started |
| End of Class 3 | IS-700 certificate or retake plan; specialization selected; first at-home action scheduled |
| End of Zombilympics | At least one common-foundation certificate and a completed, verifiable portion of the individual track |
| First of May | Cohort is ready for a supervised community-service mission with assigned ICS roles |

# Weekly credential check-in

The check-in is brief and happens every week after this module. Bring evidence, not just a percentage.

1. **Target:** What credential or skill are you pursuing?
2. **Evidence:** Show a certificate, course dashboard, practice log, map, quiz result, contact record, or completed artifact.
3. **Progress:** What changed since the last check-in?
4. **Obstacle:** What is slowing you down, and who can help?
5. **Next action:** Name one task and a date for completing it.
6. **Community role:** Explain how this work helps the cohort serve safely by May 1.

Students keep their own passwords and personal identification information private. The class records completion evidence and milestones, not account credentials.

# Teacher implementation notes

* FEMA lists IS-100.c as a two-hour course and IS-700.b as a 3.5-hour course. Both have no prerequisites, but the final exams require a FEMA SID.
* Use the downloaded instructor guides, student manuals, visuals, and handouts in this folder for discussion and review. Students should complete FEMA's current online course and official exam for an official completion certificate.
* A failed exam is feedback, not an endpoint. Record the missed objective, review it, and schedule the next legitimate attempt without sharing or reproducing protected test questions.
* Completion of training does not authorize a minor to self-deploy. All field service remains supervised and coordinated with the responsible organization.
