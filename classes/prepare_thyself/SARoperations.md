# SAR Operations

**Community role:** support a search base with accurate mapping, documentation, communications, supervised aerial observation, and medical readiness.

**Primary pathway:** CalTopo for Incident Response certificate

**Supporting pathways:** Part 107 preparation, CPR/first aid blended learning, and NASAR SARTECH preparation

Most knowledge work can begin at home. Field searching, drone missions, CPR skills testing, and operational SAR work require qualified adult supervision and organizational permission.

# Credential stack

## CalTopo for Incident Response

CalTopo—formerly familiar to many responders as SARTopo—offers a self-paced **CalTopo for Incident Response** course. It follows a multi-operational-period missing-person search and includes a free NASAR certificate-of-completion opportunity.

Course high points:

* create and organize an incident map
* use operational periods
* build line, area, and buffer assignments
* record clues and update assignment status
* import, export, share, and print map information
* support field teams with mobile maps, tracks, and location information
* maintain a usable common operating picture
* close and archive an incident

Start here:

* [CalTopo for Incident Response course](https://training.caltopo.com/firstresponse/course){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}
* [CalTopo first-response training library](https://training.caltopo.com/firstresponse){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

At-home evidence:

* NASAR/CalTopo certificate of completion
* exported practice map or PDF map packet
* screenshot of assignments, clues, and operational periods
* short explanation of the map's naming and documentation conventions

Do not place student home addresses, live locations, or other private information on shared practice maps.

## Part 107 preparation and supervised flight operations

Students may learn Part 107 content and manipulate the flight controls during an operation when a certificated remote pilot in command directly supervises them and can immediately take direct control. The adult remote PIC remains responsible for the operation.

Current FAA eligibility guidance requires a first-time Part 107 applicant to be **at least 16 years old**. The FAA also describes applicants as obtaining an FAA Tracking Number before scheduling the initial UAG knowledge test. Therefore, grades 7–8 should treat this as **test preparation and supervised mission training**, not as an early test whose result waits to activate at 16.

[FAA: Become a Certificated Remote Pilot](https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators/become_a_drone_pilot){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

[FAA Advisory Circular 107-2A](https://www.faa.gov/documentlibrary/media/advisory_circular/ac_107-2a.pdf){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

Study high points:

* airspace and operating limitations
* sectional charts and airspace authorization
* weather and performance
* loading, batteries, maintenance, and preflight inspection
* crew resource management
* emergency procedures
* night operations, people, vehicles, and Remote ID
* search-grid photography and maintaining an evidence log

At-home evidence:

* study log and practice-exam trend
* completed mission plan and risk assessment
* weather briefing and airspace analysis for a fictional mission
* image log tied to a CalTopo practice map

## CPR and basic first aid

Students can complete the knowledge portion of an approved blended course at home. A recognized CPR/AED card generally requires an in-person hands-on skills session with an authorized instructor; an online-only completion screen is not a substitute.

At-home evidence:

* completed online knowledge portion
* scheduled hands-on session
* final course-completion card after skills testing

[American Heart Association blended-learning explanation](https://cpr.heart.org/en/training-programs/elearning-and-blended-learning-courses){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

## NASAR SARTECH pathway

Use NASAR material to learn the vocabulary and expectations of professional SAR, but verify age, evaluator, prerequisite, and local-team requirements before paying for an examination. For younger students, the immediate goal may be preparation and documented competencies rather than an operational field rating.

SARTECH-related study high points:

* personal readiness and survival
* land navigation
* search tactics and clue awareness
* communications
* scene safety and subject care
* search-base forms, briefing, debriefing, and accountability

[NASAR education and certification](https://nasar.org/page/courses){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

# Zombilympics checkpoint

The preferred target is the CalTopo for Incident Response certificate plus a complete practice incident-map packet. A student pursuing medical or drone work may add an online-course completion record, scheduled skills session, or documented Part 107 study benchmark.

# May 1 cohort role

Possible supervised roles include map-unit assistant, assignment-status recorder, clue logger, radio logger, drone visual observer, imagery logger, equipment runner, and search-base documentation assistant. Students do not self-deploy, conduct unsupervised searches, or represent themselves as credentialed field responders.

# Weekly check-in evidence

Bring one current artifact: CalTopo lesson progress, map export, assignment form, study score, flight plan, risk assessment, medical-course record, or partner contact.
