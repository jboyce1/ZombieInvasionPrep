# Sandbagging Mission: Emergency Logistics

[Back to Prepare Thyself Class 1]({{ '/classes/prepare_thyself/class1' | relative_url }})

**Your challenge:** How many useful sandbags can your team produce safely—and what people, equipment, materials, and time would an emergency request require?

This mission has two parts: a supervised production trial and a planning exercise using your team's measured output. The emergency scenarios are paper exercises; the production trial takes place on a teacher-inspected dry practice site with clean sand.

## Part 1 — Measure your team's output

Work in a team of four. In 20 minutes, fill, close, carry, and place acceptable sandbags at the teacher's marked destination. Count only completed bags at that location. Record your roles, tools, carrying distance, supply interruptions, and bottlenecks.

Use the [student calculation packet (PDF)]({{ '/classes/field_assignments/files/sandbag-emergency-logistics/01-student-basic-calculation-packet.pdf' | relative_url }}) for the trial instructions, team data, calculations, and common references. For a shorter handout, use the [one-page basic calculation sheet (PDF)]({{ '/classes/field_assignments/files/sandbag-emergency-logistics/00-basic-calculation-sheet-only.pdf' | relative_url }}).

Wear gloves, safety glasses, and closed-toe footwear. Keep shovel and carry routes clear, lift comfortably, and stop for pain, fatigue, or unsafe conditions. Follow the teacher's demonstrated fill and closure standard. Report supplies that run out; do not present a shortened trial as a full 20-minute measurement.

## Part 2 — Plan an emergency response

Your teacher will assign a request from the [operations scenario bank (PDF)]({{ '/classes/field_assignments/files/sandbag-emergency-logistics/03-student-scenario-bank.pdf' | relative_url }}). Complete an [individual operational planning worksheet (PDF)]({{ '/classes/field_assignments/files/sandbag-emergency-logistics/02-student-operational-planning-worksheet.pdf' | relative_url }}).

1. Define the mission and sketch your proposed approach with dimensions, drainage, access, and movement routes.
2. Estimate bags and fill using the packet's classroom assumptions.
3. Use your measured rate and the packet's planning factors to compare completion time for one team and four teams.
4. Calculate the staffing, equipment, and materials needed to meet the deadline. Account for delivery, setup, safe working space, and bottlenecks.
5. Respond to an [incident-update card (PDF)]({{ '/classes/field_assignments/files/sandbag-emergency-logistics/04-incident-update-cards.pdf' | relative_url }}) and revise your plan.

For an extension, your teacher may assign one of the [12 creative field missions (PDF)]({{ '/classes/field_assignments/files/sandbag-emergency-logistics/06-creative-field-missions.pdf' | relative_url }}). Use the same measured production method and explain the additional assumptions your design requires.

## What to submit

* Your team's completed trial data and basic calculations.
* Your individual planning worksheet, including a sketch, material estimate, time comparison, and resource request.
* Your revised plan after an incident update, with an explanation of what changed.

Be ready to explain whether the request is achievable and what fallback you recommend if the deadline or conditions make it impractical.
