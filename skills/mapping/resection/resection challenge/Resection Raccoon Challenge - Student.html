Mission: Locate yourself and each missing Resection Raccoon. Measure azimuths clockwise from north. When given sightings from an unknown position, calculate the back azimuths and draw those lines from the named schools. Call ZULU MAIN only when your team has a defensible location.

“ZULU MAIN, this is [TEAM]. We have a location for [CALL SIGN]. The location is [DESCRIBE AND POINT OUT LOCATION]. Our fix used [LANDMARK 1] and [LANDMARK 2]. Over.”
