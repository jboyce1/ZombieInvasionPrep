Suggested ZULU MAIN response

“[TEAM], this is ZULU MAIN. Copy your fix for [CALL SIGN]. Confirm the two landmarks and repeat the location. Over.”

Ask the team to point to its intersection on the map. If the position is wrong, ask only: “Did you draw the measured azimuth or the back azimuth from the school?”

Interactive Radio Scripts

Teaching intent: Do not volunteer all four sightings at once. Students should learn that a useful resection request needs at least two identifiable landmarks and a direction or azimuth to each. Give one response per clear radio question. If they select two lines with a weak crossing angle, let them request a third sighting.

Assessment checks

  • Student identifies the landmark before requesting a bearing.
  • Student recognizes that two useful sightings are sufficient.
  • Student converts direction language to an azimuth when needed.
  • Student reverses the reported sighting before drawing from the school.
  • Student reports the fix clearly to ZULU MAIN and can defend the intersection.
