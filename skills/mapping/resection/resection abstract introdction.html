Resection: Abstract Introduction

Name/Group: Day:
Purpose. Resection finds your unknown position by using two or more visible landmarks whose positions are already known. Measure the azimuth from you to each landmark, convert each to a back azimuth, then draw those back-azimuth lines from the landmarks. Your position is where the lines intersect.

Essential ideas

Azimuth: an angle measured clockwise from north, from 000° through 360°.

Line of sight: the straight line between an observer and a visible landmark.

Back azimuth: the same line viewed in the opposite direction.

Back-azimuth rule
If azimuth is less than 180°, add 180°.
If azimuth is 180° or greater, subtract 180°.
Use 000° rather than 360° when recording north.
